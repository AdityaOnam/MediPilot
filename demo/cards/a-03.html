The six invariants
1
Asymmetric autonomy — may escalate on its own, may never lower a human-assigned band.
2
No naked scores — every value carries a confidence indicator and the inputs it used.
3
Age is never assumed — no threshold applies before a stratum resolves.
4
Freshness is part of the value — a stale reading renders as missing, not as a number.
5
Abstention is loud, and never Green — holds at the Yellow floor.
6
The human closes every loop — override, one touch, always recorded with a reason.
