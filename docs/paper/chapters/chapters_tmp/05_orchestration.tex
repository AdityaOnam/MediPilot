\section{Backend Orchestration \& Operations}

The final layer of the architecture acts upon the decisions generated by both the clinical rules and the risk engine.

\subsection{Band Engine}
The Band Engine is responsible for consolidating the outputs from the deterministic rule-cards (e.g., Red-Flags, safety floors) and the GBDT model. It determines the patient's final acuity band (Red, Yellow, or Green) based on a strict precedence hierarchy, ensuring that model probabilities never override hard clinical safety boundaries.

\subsection{Surge Controller}
In high-stress emergency department environments, bed capacity can be overwhelmed. The Surge Controller monitors overall hospital capacity and dynamically applies surge policies. During severe overload, it can restrict the assignment of the Red band, capping the number of patients permitted in the critical queue and forcing non-critical patients downward, ensuring that only the highest acuity cases receive immediate attention.

\subsection{Recheck Scheduler}
Patients placed in lower acuity bands or waiting in queues require continuous monitoring. The Recheck Scheduler uses age-stratified cadence matrices (configured in \texttt{band\_cadence.yaml}) to automatically calculate when a nurse must manually remeasure vital signs. The cadence logic adjusts dynamically based on the staleness of the current vitals and the patient's assigned band.
